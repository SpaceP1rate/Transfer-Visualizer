# Body maps

Both are NASA imagery in the public domain, resized to 2048x1024 equirectangular
and re-encoded as WebP (about 210 KB and 240 KB).

- `earth.webp` — NASA Visible Earth "Blue Marble" colour map, taken from the
  `three-globe` package (`example/img/earth-blue-marble.jpg`, 4096x2048).
- `moon.webp` — NASA/LRO lunar surface albedo map, taken from the `globe.gl`
  package (`example/moon-landing-sites/lunar_surface.jpg`, 11469x5734).

Neither is used for measurement — they are surface decoration on the two body
spheres, which are drawn at their true radii.
